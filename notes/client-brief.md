# Client brief: Kenyon College Finance Club (KCFC)

## About the club

The Kenyon College Finance Club is a student organization founded in 2019 that helps students explore finance and prepare for opportunities in the field. The club offers stock pitches, trading competitions, speaker events, resume workshops, and an Analyst Program for newer members. Its alumni network also connects students with people working at banks and investment firms and has helped members find employment opportunities.

This year, the club has three presidents: **Aishik Biswas, Lucas Flynn, and Madeline Ha**. The three of us organize the club's activities and coordinate its meetings and events. We meet **every other week in Oden 110**, and our primary means of communication is the club email, **financeclub@kenyon.edu**. Students can also fill out forms to request a resume review.

One of our main events is the spring stock pitch competition. Members work in three groups, each covering a different sector and led by one of the three presidents. The groups then present their pitches to Kenyon alumni working in equity research at **William Blair**. This gives members an opportunity to apply what they have learned and receive feedback from people working in the industry.

## Who would manage the website

The website needs to reflect how the club is actually organized. Since the three presidents share responsibility for running the club, all three should have administrator access. This would allow us to update official club information, maintain the Leadership and Analyst Program pages, and manage accounts for other officers.

Other officers should be able to contribute event announcements, stock pitch writeups, recruiting resources, and alumni event recaps. These contributions should be straightforward to create and update, without requiring someone to learn a complicated editing system. The presidents would remain responsible for managing access and reviewing material that represents the club publicly.

The website also needs to be manageable when leadership changes. Each incoming officer should receive an individual account with the appropriate permissions, and outgoing officers should lose access when their responsibilities end. This would make the transition less dependent on a shared password or on one person knowing how the website works.

## Who would use the website

- **Prospective members**, especially first-years and sophomores, would want to understand what the club does, whether they need prior experience, and when and where meetings take place.
- **Current members** would use it to find event details, recruiting resources, resume review forms, and information about the Analyst Program and spring stock pitch competition.
- **Alumni and recruiters** would use it to learn about the club's leadership and activities before speaking at an event, reviewing a pitch, or helping a student with recruiting.

## Problems with the current website

The current Wix website, financeclub15.wixsite.com, does not adequately represent the club's activities. It has no current officers, meeting times, or event announcements listed, and the Contact Us link leads back to the homepage. The Resources section contains headings without material beneath them. For someone unfamiliar with the club, this could suggest that it is no longer active.

Most of our current information is communicated through email. While this works for announcements and direct communication, it also means that members have to look through earlier messages to find meeting details or resources. The website could provide a consistent place to access that information, alongside links to forms such as the resume review request form.

Updating the site is also difficult when only one or two people have access. A system that allows officers to contribute within clearly defined roles would make regular updates more manageable. The Wix subdomain and advertising banner also limit how professionally the club presents itself to alumni and prospective members.

## What an improved website should provide

- A simple way for officers to create event announcements, ideally in under two minutes.
- A homepage that clearly displays our meeting schedule, location, contact email, and upcoming events.
- Accessible links to resume review forms and relevant recruiting resources.
- An archive of reviewed stock pitches and alumni event recaps that future members can learn from.
- Individual accounts and clear permissions, with an uncomplicated process for transferring responsibility to new leadership.

## Access and publishing requirements

- **Draft stock pitches must remain private until they have been reviewed.** Since members present their work to alumni, material published under the club's name should be complete and checked.
- **Only the three presidents, as administrators, should be able to edit or delete the Leadership and Analyst Program pages.** Other officers can contribute content without having control over these official pages.
- **Outgoing officers should no longer be able to log in once their roles end.** Access should reflect a person's current responsibilities within the club.
- **Only the presidents should be able to create accounts or change user roles.** These restrictions need to be enforced by the system, including when someone attempts to access a restricted page directly.
