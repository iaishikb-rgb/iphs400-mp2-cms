About

The Kenyon College Finance Club is a student organization, founded in 2019, that helps students explore finance and prepare for opportunities in the field.

We run stock pitches, trading competitions, speaker events and resume workshops, and an Analyst Program for newer members. Our alumni network connects students with people at banks and investment firms, and has helped members find jobs.

We meet every other week in Oden 110. The best way to reach us is financeclub@kenyon.edu.
